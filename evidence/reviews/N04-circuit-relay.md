# N04 increment: replaceable Circuit Relay v2 transport

Full N04 remains open until actual NAT/firewall, AutoNAT/DCUtR, native configuration and resource accounting/expiry scenarios pass. This increment adds real one-hop circuits to the existing signed route and immutable MLS outbox path. Loopback process tests are not a NAT claim.

Contract before production:

- Node serve accepts explicit --relay-server with bounded --relay-capacity1..16 (default16), repeated --relay direct IP/TCP or QUIC peer addresses (max4 distinct relays), and --relay-only requiring at least one relay. Normal daemon does not serve other peers' circuits unless opted in. Relay-only advertises confirmed circuit routes and restricts application dialing to them; it is a reachability/test mode, not an anonymity guarantee.
- Direct routes remain supported. Circuit routes are exactly direct endpoint + relay PeerID + p2p-circuit + destination PeerID, one hop, bounded by the same8routes/256bytes limits. Mixed destination IDs, malformed/nested routes are rejected before contact/history/outbox mutation. Root-to-transport identity binding remains mandatory at the final recipient.
- Existing delivery CBOR is transported over the libp2p circuit secured end-to-end by Noise and application MLS. Relays have only their own transport/profile identity. They do not join conversations or receive application plaintext/keys.
- Maintain bounded reservations with retry/backoff after failed/closed listeners, independently of pending messages. Advertise only confirmed, currently valid routes. Two configured relay processes allow failover after the active one is killed. If all disappear, immutable sender outbox survives restart and recovers without a new operation or invitation.
- Server limits: at most16reservations, one established connection per peer in server mode, at most32circuits globally, finite120second/1MiB circuit caps, finite300second reservation TTL, existing upstream per-peer/per-IP rate limits and bounded swarm connections. Capacity is reclaimed after disconnect; an excess reservation cannot evict established conversations. Actual byte/TTL exhaustion and renewal matrix are subsequent required tests, not asserted complete here.
- Owner-only node_info exposes direct listeners separately from confirmed relayRoutes and active peerConnections (actual remote address and relayed flag), plus bounded server reservation/circuit counters. No plaintext/keys in diagnostics. Relay lists are CLI inputs in this increment; native persistent settings are still required.

Tests first: real subprocesses exchange authenticated invitation/MLS Welcome, message/reply, signed receipt; inspect a single unambiguous active peer circuit; kill the active relay, then both; restart sender and surviving relay, assert autonomous delivery of the original operation/history/receipt before any repeated send. Providers use different underlying TCP/QUIC transports, requiring both to deliver. Capacity2 with a third independent client proves rejection, continuity and autonomous admission after disconnect. Signed invalid circuit routes leave snapshot/outbox unchanged; valid control is accepted. Ordinary daemon rejects reservation until operator restarts with relay-server opt-in; waiting client recovers automatically and sends an actual message.
